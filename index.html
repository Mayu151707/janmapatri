Learn to read a Kundli

Short explanations for key Vedic astrology concepts.

1 · Lagna / Ascendant

The sign rising on the eastern horizon at birth. Traditionally used as the chart's starting point and reference for houses.

2 · Moon sign (Rashi)

The zodiac sign occupied by the Moon at birth. In Vedic astrology, it is a central reference for emotional themes and many daily readings.

3 · Nakshatra

The lunar mansion segment occupied by a planet. The Moon's Nakshatra is used in the traditional Vimshottari Dasha starting sequence.

4 · Houses (Bhavas)

Twelve chart divisions associated with different life topics in traditional interpretation. This prototype uses whole-sign houses.

5 · Navamsa (D9)

A divisional chart derived by dividing each zodiac sign into nine parts. It is traditionally used for additional interpretive detail.

6 · Dasha

Planetary-period system used to organize traditional timing interpretations. The sequence is derived from the Moon's Nakshatra at birth.

7 · Yoga

Specific planetary combinations described in classical astrology. Yoga results should be identified from actual chart conditions, not generic lists.

8 · Ayanamsa

The offset between tropical and sidereal zodiac reference frames. This prototype uses Lahiri ayanamsa.
